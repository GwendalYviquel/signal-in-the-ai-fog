## Editorial line

For leaders and engineers who run enterprise AI platforms, with a European perspective and an interest in retail. No basic explanations, no gratuitous jargon. Angles stay generic ("for a platform team", "for a retailer"): the audience is never identifiable. The full line is in [prompt.md](prompt.md).

Themes, in segment order (`show.yaml`): Models and vendors, Sovereignty and Europe, Platform, Quality, Regulation, Retail and agents.

## Rhythm of the week

- **Monday**: a summary of the whole weekend, from Friday evening to Sunday, and What to watch covers the week ahead.
- **Tuesday to Thursday**: news of the last 24 to 72 hours.
- **Friday**: the story of the day steps back to a trend of the week.

## History
