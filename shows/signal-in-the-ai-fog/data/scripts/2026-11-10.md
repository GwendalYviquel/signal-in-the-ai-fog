# Anthropic's IPO price missing, EuroHPC dates move

_2026-11-10_

Anthropic's IPO marketing reportedly starts next week, and its amended registration filing still shows no price range. Also: EuroHPC moves its AI gigafactory deadlines and orders bidders to resubmit, OpenAI's new GPT-6 family pages, and an agentic commerce startup funding round.

## Script

Good morning, it's Tuesday, November 10. This is Signal in the AI Fog, your daily briefing on enterprise AI in production, written and read by AI. Today: Anthropic IPO filing still shows no price range, EuroHPC gigafactory deadlines shift, and Agentic commerce startup raises fifteen million dollars.

First, the story of the day.

Anthropic's registration statement history shows an original filing on October twenty-sixth and one amendment on November second. Neither contains a price range. According to The Information, which reported on November sixth, the company planned to proceed with formal investor marketing the following week. Reports point to a listing around November sixteenth, which Anthropic has not confirmed. Anthropic declined to comment. The price range is not public.

What this really signals: a roadshow can start before a price range is public, so timing is soft. For a platform team, the amended filing is the document to read. Look for capacity commitments, dependence on a few cloud partners, and risk factors about pricing. Nothing changes in your contract today. Use the wait to rehearse a failover of production traffic to another vendor through your gateway, and record how long it took. Worth watching: whether a price range appears, and whether the reported November sixteenth date holds.

Next, Models and vendors.

OpenAI has published two new launch pages in the past week: one introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna, the other introducing GPT-6.1 Terra. I could not review the details of either page, so I give no prices, regions or availability. Treat both as announcements until your own account shows them callable.

The bigger picture: the GPT-6 family now has several named tiers, and names tell you little. For a platform team, add each as a candidate route only once price, retirement date and data terms are written down. Then test it on your own evaluation set.

Next, Sovereignty and Europe.

On November sixth, EuroHPC announced that bidders for its AI gigafactories call must recreate and resubmit their tenders, because of a technical issue in the submission system. This affects both lots, drafts included. New submission links are promised in the coming days. The medium-scale lot now closes on February fourth, 2027, so member states can finish budget procedures. The large-scale lot closes on December third, 2026. Both at five p.m. Central European Time.

Reading between the lines: the November twelfth date no longer applies, and the medium-scale slip suggests national financing is the slow part. For a platform team, nothing here is rentable yet, and it likely stays that way for years. Worth watching which consortia name model and cloud partners.

Next, Retail and agents.

On November fifth, the startup firmly announced a fifteen million dollar Series A round. It also launched Ask Agent, a product it says helps merchants adopt agentic commerce more easily. This comes from the company's own press release. I have no independent figures on customers or usage.

What this really signals: money is flowing to the layer between merchants and shopping agents. For a retailer, the build or buy question is open. Either way, product data, stock accuracy and APIs that an agent can read are the real prerequisites. Check those first.

The number of the day.

February fourth, 2027. That is the new deadline for medium-scale AI gigafactory bids, extended so member states can complete national budget procedures. Large-scale bids are now due December third, 2026.

What to watch.

Anthropic's amended filing with a price range, and the reported November sixteenth listing. New EuroHPC submission links. Microsoft Ignite and Google's Sovereign AI Summit from November seventeenth. OVHcloud's summit and Gemini 3.6 Flash retirement on November nineteenth. Walmart's Agent Profiles, due by about November twenty-first. AI Act marking on December second. Then EuroHPC's large-scale deadline on December third.

And a question for your team.

If your main model vendor's financial disclosures, pricing or availability changed next month, which route would take the traffic, and who on your team would decide to switch?

That's it for today. Sources are in the episode description. This was Signal in the AI Fog. See you tomorrow.
